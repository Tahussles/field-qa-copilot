Field QA Copilot
Prototype Built for community data collection teams

Catch fabricated field data before it reaches the analysis.

When people are rewarded per submission, some submissions get invented. The copilot checks every interview as it arrives, explains what looks wrong, and hands reviewers a short list instead of a spreadsheet.

Household Water Access Survey · Kisumu, wave 2Checking submissions
Each dot is an interview arriving · orange means flagged
01 · Check

Six checks on every interview

Too fast, same spot, copied answers, straight-lining, voice note vs form, and off-hours. Cheap, explainable, run the moment data syncs.

02 · Explain

Evidence, not a black box

Each flag shows why: the cluster on a map, the two copied answers side by side, the voice note that contradicts the form.

03 · Decide

A human makes every call

Reviewers approve, reject or send a supervisor to back-check. A morning brief tells the lead researcher who to look at first.

Synthetic data with fraud planted on purpose, so every check can be scored. Prototype by Taha Hussain.
